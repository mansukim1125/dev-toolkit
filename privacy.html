Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This page describes how information is handled when you use Dev Toolkit.

Tool inputs and files

Dev Toolkit's conversion, formatting, and parsing tools process the text and files you provide in your browser. The tool code does not upload those inputs to an application server or save them in cookies or browser storage.

Copy buttons write results to your clipboard when you request it. Download buttons save generated files to your device.

Hosting and external libraries

The site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. Some tools load JavaScript libraries from jsDelivr. When your browser requests pages or external resources, the service providing them receives network information such as your IP address and browser request headers.

These providers handle information according to their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • jsDelivr Privacy Policy

Advertising and cookies

The Dev Toolkit home page loads Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure advertisements, including personalized ads depending on your settings and applicable consent choices.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage advertising preferences in Google My Ad Center and manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to this page

This page may be updated when the site's features or services change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
